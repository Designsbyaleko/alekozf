Light Rail Safety Campaign

Purpose

This campaign raises awareness of reckless driving around the Canberra Light Rail, with a focus on young drivers aged 17–24. Using vibrant pop-art colours, sequential storytelling, and a catchy slogan, the visuals educate motorists on safe habits and highlight real crash statistics.

Meeting the Brief

I followed a clear process from research to final artwork. The brief asked for a bold campaign that would warn young drivers about reckless behaviour near Canberra’s Light Rail. Drivers aged 17–24 cause one quarter of serious road injury crashes, so the message had to hit home. I used pop art colours for instant impact and sequential art panels to tell a simple story. A short, memorable slogan ties it together and urges drivers to “think twice before being hasty.” The result meets every requirement: it is eye catching, easy to understand, and ready for posters and socials.

Golf Adventure Park Identity

Purpose

The brief was to create a complete brand identity for a local mini-golf adventure park, attracting families and young adults with a fresh, fun look. Deliverables included logo, colour system, typography, merchandise, signage, social templates, and a responsive website prototype.

Meeting the Brief

The goal was to give a local mini-golf park a fresh look that would attract families and young adults. I started by defining the brand personality: fun, friendly, and outdoorsy. From there I built a logo with rounded letterforms and a playful golf ball mascot. A bright green palette signals energy and nature, while simple icons guide visitors around the course and on the website. The full logo, colours, type, signage, merch, and a booking ready web mock-up, delivers a consistent brand voice across every touch point and gives the park a clear, modern identity.
