About Me

I'm Alexander Fitzsimons, a Graphic Designer passionate about building purposeful and clear visual experiences. Based in Canberra, I focus on crafting designs that connect ideas to people through clarity, creativity, and thoughtful execution.

I'm currently studying Visual Communication Design at the University of Canberra, where I focus on developing strong foundations in interaction design, graphic design, and user experience. I'm passionate about creating innovative, thoughtful solutions that connect ideas to people. My approach blends creativity with practical problem-solving, aiming to deliver designs that are not only visually strong but also meaningful and effective.

My work spans across branding, digital design, and user-centered visual communication. Whether I'm developing a visual identity, designing a website, or creating marketing assets, my goal remains the same: to deliver clear, considered, and engaging outcomes that meet real needs.
